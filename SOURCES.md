## Editor notes
- BOI: do not cite pre-2025 “domestic LLC must file BOI” guidance as current. Prefer current FinCEN/Treasury materials.
- Privacy: frame CCPA/CPRA as state law, not a single federal general privacy statute.
- Sales tax: Wayfair enables state economic nexus; thresholds vary by state.
- DOL Feb. 2026 IC NPRM: proposed only.
- Avoid inventing exact fees where schedules change.
- This source list is not legal advice.
