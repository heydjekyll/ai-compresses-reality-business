# AI Compresses Execution Time. It Doesn't Compress Reality.

A documented breakdown of what “building a company in 3 days” can—and cannot—mean when AI dramatically accelerates execution.

> **AI compresses execution time. It doesn't compress reality.**

This repository contains the full article and the source list used for the analysis.

## Contents

- [ARTICLE.md](ARTICLE.md) — full article
- [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) — source list and research notes

## Why this repository exists

The article separates what AI and automation can accelerate from the parts of building a business that still depend on real-world validation, legal and financial processes, people, customers, operations, and time.

The goal is simple: make the underlying reasoning and sources easy to inspect, verify, and share.

## Origin

The article was prompted by the claim:

> “Building a company in 3 days — launching today!”

The claim is treated as the starting point for analysis, not as the subject of a personal attack.
